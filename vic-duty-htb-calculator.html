First home buyer exemption and concession

  • Dutiable value $600,000 or less → duty is nil.
  • $600,001 – $750,000 → sliding concession. The SRO formula is:
    duty = full duty × (dutiable value − 600,000) ÷ 150,000
    So at $675,000 you pay half the full duty; at $700,000 the result is $24,713.
  • Above $750,000 → no concession, full general rates apply. There is no cliff at $750,000: the factor reaches 1.0 exactly there, so duty already equals the full rate. The sting is inside the band, where the marginal rate on each extra dollar of price runs around 23–27%.
  • The concession is worked out on the general rates, not the lower principal-place-of-residence rates.

Principal place of residence concession

For owner-occupiers who are not first home buyers, a lower rate of $2,870 + 5% over $130,000 applies, but only up to a dutiable value of $550,000. Above $550,000 everyone pays general rates.

Land and construction

Duty is charged on the land value only, not the build contract, provided you build and move in within the SRO time limits. That is why a $420,000 block plus a $420,000 build attracts no duty for a first home buyer, even though the finished home is worth $840,000.

Help to Buy

  • Government equity share up to 30% for existing homes, 40% for new builds. Minimum 2% deposit. No LMI.
  • Victorian price cap: $950,000 in Melbourne and Geelong; $650,000–$700,000 elsewhere in Victoria.
  • Income test: about $103,000 individual, $165,000 joint or single parent, on the prior year's ATO notice of assessment.
  • The government shares proportionally in any gain or loss when you sell or buy out its share.

5% Deposit Scheme

  • Since 1 October 2025: no income caps, no limit on places, and the Victorian price cap lifted to $950,000 in Melbourne and Geelong ($650,000 elsewhere).
  • The government guarantees part of the loan so no LMI is charged. It is a guarantee, not a grant — you borrow and repay the full amount.
  • For land and construction on separate contracts, land price plus build cost together must be under the cap.

What this does not cover

Foreign purchaser additional duty (8%), the off-the-plan concession, pensioner concessions, the $10,000 First Home Owner Grant on new homes, land tax, or lender fees. Duty figures are estimates — the SRO calculator and your conveyancer give the assessable amount.
